# About Me

## Hey, I'm Anup! 👋

Cybersecurity professional who just never stopped asking "but how does it actually work? I've spent the last 6+ years poking holes in systems, chasing vulnerabilities, and learning something new every single time something breaks. This blog is where I'll dump my notes, share my findings, and hopefully make your security journey a little less painful and a lot more fun.

## How I Got Here

Honestly? I'm just always being curious. What started as "hmm, I wonder how this works" turned into a full-blown career in Cyber security and penetration testing. Along the way I picked up my **OSCP**, **CRTP**, and **CPTS** — each one a grind, each one worth it.

These days you'll find me on **Hack The Box** and **TryHackMe**, either working through machines or just enjoying the chaos of a good challenge.

## What You'll Find Here

- 🔍 Penetration testing tips and walkthroughs
- 🧠 Offensive security concepts and red teaming ideas
- 🚩 HTB / THM write-ups and CTF breakdowns
- 🛠️ Tools, tricks, and techniques that actually work in the real world

No fluff, no filler. Just things I'd want to read myself.

## Outside the Terminal

When I'm not breaking things digitally, I'm probably travelling somewhere new, making friends I'll keep for life, or listening to music loud enough to drown out everything else. I also volunteer whenever I get the chance because giving back genuinely matters to me, whether that's mentoring newcomers in the security space or contributing to community initiatives.

I believe the best ideas come from stepping away from the screen once in a while.

## Let's Connect!

I love hearing from people — questions, collabs, or just a good old "hey." You can find me on [GitHub](https://anuprm.github.io/), [Twitter](https://x.com/anup_rm), and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/anup-m-75aa56222/), and if you're on HTB or THM, maybe I'll see you on the leaderboard. 😄

Glad you're here. Stick around, there's plenty to explore. 🔐
